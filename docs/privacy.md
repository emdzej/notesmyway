# Privacy

*Last updated 6 November 2026.*

Notes my Way collects nothing about you. The one exception is a problem report you choose to send.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.** The app sends nothing to me or to anyone else unless you send a problem report (below).
- **Your notes stay where you put them.** Notes are files in folders you choose. The app reads and writes them there and keeps a search index in its own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes the index, not your notes.
- **Link previews are the only network access.** For links you mark with `{.preview}`, the app fetches that page to show its title, description and image. Nothing else is fetched, and the notebook setting **Load previews for web links** turns it off entirely. Fetched previews are cached on your device.
- **Problem reports are sent only when you send one.** **Report a Problem…** (the Help menu on the Mac, the notebook menu on iPhone and iPad) sends what you write to Notes my Way's support desk, `help.getalias.support`, run with [ALIAS](https://getalias.support). With it go the app version, the platform, the app language, the editor mode, which kind of screen was open, and the number of notes and notebooks (you can untick the counts). **No note titles, text, file or folder names are sent.** A screenshot of the window is added only if you add it, and you can black out parts of it first; it's sent without its metadata. Email addresses you type in a report are masked before it leaves the device. There's no account: the desk gives the install an anonymous ID, kept on your device (in the Keychain, or on the Mac in the app's own storage), so you can read the answers in **Your Reports**. Resolved reports are deleted after a year. Your IP address reaches the desk as with any web request and is used only to limit abuse. Reports are not linked to you, not used for tracking and not shared.
- **Sync** is whatever you use for the folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, …); their own policies apply.
- **Links** in notes open in your browser, where those sites' policies apply. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
