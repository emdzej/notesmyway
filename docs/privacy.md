# Privacy

*Last updated 29 October 2026.*

Notes my Way collects nothing about you.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.** The app contains no third-party SDKs and sends nothing to me or to anyone else.
- **Your notes stay where you put them.** Notes are files in folders you choose. The app reads and writes them there and keeps a search index in its own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes the index, not your notes.
- **Link previews are the only network access.** For links you mark with `{.preview}`, the app fetches that page to show its title, description and image. Nothing else is fetched, and the notebook setting **Load previews for web links** turns it off entirely. Fetched previews are cached on your device.
- **Sync** is whatever you use for the folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, …); their own policies apply.
- **Links** in notes open in your browser, where those sites' policies apply. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/emdzej/notesmyway/issues).
