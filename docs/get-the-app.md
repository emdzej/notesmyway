Notes my Way is being prepared for the App Store. This page will link to it as soon as it's there.

## Mac: download now

The Mac app is ready to download from GitHub: [**Download for Mac**](https://github.com/emdzej/notesmyway/releases/latest) (`Notes-my-Way-0.1.0.dmg`). Open the DMG and drag Notes my Way into Applications. It's signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a double-click.

When the App Store version arrives, it reads the same notebooks: your notes are files in your folders, not in the app.

## Open source

Notes my Way is free software under the [GPLv3](https://github.com/emdzej/notesmyway/blob/main/LICENSE). The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/emdzej/notesmyway), with the editor, [MarkdownKit](https://github.com/emdzej/markdownkit), in its own repository. Bug reports and pull requests are welcome.

## What you'll need

| Device | System |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone | iOS 26 or later |
| iPad | iPadOS 26 or later |
| Mac | macOS 26 or later |

No account, no sign-up. You'll need a folder of Markdown files — or an empty folder to start one. It can be in iCloud Drive, on your device, or anywhere the Files app and Finder can reach.
