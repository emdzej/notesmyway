# Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app?

- **Read the guide first:** [User guide](/guide) and [Markdown in the editor](/markdown).
- **Report a bug or ask a question:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/emdzej/notesmyway/issues). Mention the device, the system version and what you expected to happen.

## Common questions

**Where are my notes?**
In the folder you opened as a notebook — they're ordinary `.md` files. The app doesn't keep a copy.

**Can I use it with Obsidian?**
Yes. Open the vault as a notebook. See [Files and compatibility](/compatibility) for what differs.

**Does it sync?**
Through whatever syncs the folder: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing, git. Built-in sync is on the [roadmap](/backlog).
