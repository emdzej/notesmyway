# Spotlight, Shortcuts and widgets

Notes my Way works with the rest of your Mac, iPhone and iPad: your notes show up in Spotlight, Shortcuts can create and read them, widgets keep pinned and recent notes in view, and the share sheet saves things from other apps.

## Spotlight

Every note in your notebooks is searchable from Spotlight, by its title, its text and its tags. Choose a result to open the note.

Spotlight stays up to date as you write, rename, move and delete notes, including changes made in other apps while Notes my Way was closed. Removing a notebook from Notes my Way removes its notes from Spotlight.

## Shortcuts and Siri

The Shortcuts app has these Notes my Way actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Note** | Creates a note with a title and text, in a notebook and folder you choose (or the notebook selected in the app). With a title the note starts with it as a heading and the file is named after it. |
| **Append to Note** | Adds text at the end of a note. |
| **Open Note** | Opens a note in the app. |
| **Search Notes** | Finds notes using the app's search, including `tag:` and `path:` filters, and returns them. |
| **Get Note Text** | Returns a note's Markdown, ready to pass to other actions. |

Ask Siri, for example, “Create a note in Notes my Way” or “Search Notes my Way”.

## Widgets

Add **Pinned Notes** or **Recent Notes** to your Home Screen, desktop or Notification Center, in small, medium or large. On iPhone they also fit the Lock Screen. Tap a note to open it, or the pencil to start a new one.

Widgets show notes from all your notebooks and update when your notes change.

## Share sheet

Choose **Notes my Way** in the share sheet of Safari, Photos or any app that shares text, a web link or images (up to five at a time). Pick the notebook, then:

- **New Note** creates a note, named after the title (a web page's title by default).
- **Add to Inbox** adds the content to `Inbox.md` at the top of the notebook, under today's date, so you can sort it later.

Add a comment if you like. Web links become [preview cards](/guide/images), and images are saved with the note like dropped files. The content arrives the next time Notes my Way is open, usually right away.

## Links

Other apps and automations can open Notes my Way with links:

- `notesmyway://new?text=Hello` creates a note (optionally with `notebook` and `folder`) and opens it.
- `notesmyway://open?notebook=…&path=…` opens a note. Widgets and Shortcuts use these.
